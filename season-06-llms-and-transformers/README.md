## Introduction

Make large language models legible: what a transformer computes, how models are pretrained, aligned and fine-tuned, how inference really works (prefill, decode, KV cache), and how to reason about model behavior, limits and selection. It is the conceptual foundation for Seasons 7–12.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Transformer Architecture** (closing episode S06E08, Vision Transformers and Modern Vision Backbones): Explain patch embedding and ViT behavior, and choose between CNN, ViT and hierarchical backbones for a task.
- **Module 2 — Training and Adapting LLMs** (closing episode S06E13, Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning: LoRA and QLoRA): Configure a LoRA/QLoRA run conceptually and justify fine-tuning versus alternatives.
- **Module 3 — Inference and Efficiency** (closing episode S06E19, Mixture of Experts and Alternatives to Dense Transformers): Explain how MoE and state-space models differ from dense transformers and reason about their serving implications.
- **Module 4 — Model Behavior and Selection** (closing episode S06E24, Multimodal LLMs: Vision-Language and Audio Models): Describe how a multimodal model ingests images and when pipelines beat native models.
