## Introduction

Teach the infrastructure that AI workloads actually run on: GPU hardware and interconnects, containers and Kubernetes configured for accelerators, distributed training and inference systems, cloud AI services, infrastructure as code, multi-tenant AI platforms, FinOps and GPU observability. DevOps, cloud and Kubernetes topics appear only as they serve AI/ML engineering; fundamentals were introduced in Seasons 1 and 11.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Compute, Memory, and Networking for AI** (closing episode S13E04, AI Accelerators and Choosing Hardware): Justify a hardware choice using memory, bandwidth and software considerations.
- **Module 2 — Containers and Kubernetes for AI** (closing episode S13E08, Batch and Training Scheduling: Queues, Gang Scheduling, and Ray): Design a scheduling policy for a shared GPU cluster.
- **Module 3 — Distributed Training Infrastructure** (closing episode S13E11, Training Infrastructure: Storage, Checkpointing, and Fault Tolerance): Design storage, checkpoint and recovery policy for a multi-day training job.
- **Module 4 — Inference Infrastructure** (closing episode S13E15, Model Artifacts: Storage, Registries, and Distribution): Plan model artifact storage and distribution to meet start-up time and security goals.
- **Module 5 — Cloud, Platform, and Operations** (closing episode S13E22, Observability for AI Infrastructure: GPU, Cluster, and Serving Metrics): Define dashboards and alerts that reveal both infrastructure health and serving efficiency.
