## Introduction

Apply AI, LLMs and agents to operations: observe, investigate, decide, remediate, deliver and govern, always with evidence, human control and measurable value. This is the "AI for DevOps" direction; operating AI and ML systems ("DevOps for AI") is covered in Seasons 11–14. The season assumes DevOps and SRE fundamentals (outside the AI-first curriculum) and the AI building blocks of Seasons 4 and 6–10; it reuses them by prerequisite and does not re-teach RAG, agents, MCP, evaluation, guardrails, MLOps or LLMOps. The AIOps overview is S14E14 and the integrative case study is S14E20; this season provides the depth behind both.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — AI-Powered DevOps Foundations** (closing episode S15E04, AI-Assisted Engineering Workflows for Operations Teams): Adopt AI assistance for operations tasks with validation, review and secret-handling rules.
- **Module 2 — Intelligent Observability** (closing episode S15E08, Natural-Language Observability and Intelligent Dashboards): Design a natural-language observability assistant with validation, limits and evidence display.
- **Module 3 — AI-Powered Incident Management** (closing episode S15E12, Incident Response Copilots: Roles, ChatOps, and Safe Assistance Under Pressure): Specify the roles, interfaces and safety rules of an incident copilot.
- **Module 4 — AI SRE & Reliability Engineering** (closing episode S15E15, AI-Assisted Capacity Planning and Reliability Recommendations): Create capacity and reliability recommendations with forecasts, evidence and an ownership workflow.
- **Module 5 — AI DevOps Agents** (closing episode S15E19, Human-Approved Remediation: Proposals, Dry Runs, Approvals, and Rollback): Specify a remediation workflow with proposals, dry runs, approvals, verification and rollback.
- **Module 6 — AI-Powered CI/CD** (closing episode S15E23, AI-Assisted Release Management and Progressive Delivery Decisions): Design AI-assisted release and canary decisions with verification and accountability.
- **Module 7 — AI Infrastructure Automation & FinOps** (closing episode S15E26, Cost Anomaly Detection and Intelligent Cloud Operations): Design a cost-anomaly and cloud-triage system with grounded explanations and ownership routing.
- **Module 8 — Enterprise AIOps Architecture** (closing episode S15E32, Evaluating and Adopting AIOps: Metrics, Shadow Mode, and Business Value): Build an evidence-based evaluation and adoption plan for an AIOps capability.
