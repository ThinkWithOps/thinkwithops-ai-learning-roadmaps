## Introduction

Cover the engineering of agent systems beyond one loop: multi-agent coordination and protocols, identity and trust, advanced planning and learning, durable long-running execution, evaluation at scale, cost economics, and enterprise governance. Assumes Season 9.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Multi-Agent Systems** (closing episode S10E04, Agent Identity, Delegation, and Trust Boundaries): Design an identity and delegation model for agents with traceable, narrowing authority.
- **Module 2 — Reasoning, Memory, and Learning** (closing episode S10E07, Learning from Feedback: Reflection, Preference Data, and Agent Fine-Tuning): Plan a safe improvement loop for an agent using logged trajectories and evaluations.
- **Module 3 — Reliability and Economics** (closing episode S10E10, Cost, Latency, and Token Economics of Agents): Model the cost of an agent workflow and apply optimizations without hurting task success.
- **Module 4 — Agent Systems in the Enterprise** (closing episode S10E15, Capstone: Designing an Enterprise Multi-Agent System): Present and defend an end-to-end enterprise multi-agent design, including where not to use agents.
