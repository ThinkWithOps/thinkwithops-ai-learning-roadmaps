## Introduction

Teach neural networks as trainable computational graphs: how they represent data, how backpropagation and optimizers train them, how vision (including video and document understanding), speech and audio, generative models and reinforcement learning work, and how to debug and scale training. It prepares learners directly for transformers and LLMs in Season 6.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Neural Network Foundations** (closing episode S05E03, Loss Functions and Output Layers for Deep Learning): Choose the output layer and loss for a given task without numerical or shape mistakes.
- **Module 2 — Training Deep Networks** (closing episode S05E08, Debugging Training: Loss Curves, Overfitting a Batch, NaNs, and Mixed Precision): Apply a repeatable debugging procedure to a non-learning model.
- **Module 3 — Computer Vision** (closing episode S05E11, Object Detection and Segmentation: IoU, NMS, and YOLO-Style Pipelines): Explain a detection pipeline end to end and interpret mAP.
- **Module 4 — Sequences and Representation** (closing episode S05E13, Embeddings and Representation Learning): Explain how embeddings are learned, compared and evaluated.
- **Module 5 — Applied Vision: Video and Documents** (closing episode S05E15, OCR and Document Intelligence): Design a document-extraction pipeline, choose between OCR, layout models and vision-language models, and measure its accuracy.
- **Module 6 — Audio and Speech** (closing episode S05E17, Speech Recognition and Speech Synthesis: CTC, Encoder-Decoder ASR, TTS, and Vocoders): Choose and evaluate an ASR or TTS approach for an application and describe its failure and misuse risks.
- **Module 7 — Generative Models** (closing episode S05E21, Diffusion Models II: Sampling, Guidance, Latent Diffusion, and Conditioning): Trace text-to-image generation end to end and reason about step count, guidance and conditioning trade-offs.
- **Module 8 — Reinforcement Learning** (closing episode S05E24, Deep RL in Practice: DQN, Reward Design, Simulation, and Offline RL): Decide when RL is appropriate, design rewards and simulators responsibly, and evaluate RL results with statistical care.
- **Module 9 — Representation Learning and Training at Scale** (closing episode S05E28, Deep Learning Project Workflow: Baselines, Evaluation, and Reproducibility): Plan and document a deep-learning experiment so results can be trusted and reproduced.
