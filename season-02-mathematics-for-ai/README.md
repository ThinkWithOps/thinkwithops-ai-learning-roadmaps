## Introduction

Teach only the mathematics that later seasons use, visually and with code, always tied to an ML consequence ("why does this matter when a model trains?"). Proofs are replaced by geometric and numerical intuition; correctness is preserved.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Linear Algebra: The Language of Data** (closing episode S02E05, Norms, Distance Metrics, and Numerical Conditioning): Select a metric and diagnose an ill-conditioned problem.
- **Module 2 — Calculus and Optimization: How Models Learn** (closing episode S02E09, Automatic Differentiation and Computational Graphs): Trace a backward pass on a small graph and explain training memory behavior.
- **Module 3 — Probability: Reasoning Under Uncertainty** (closing episode S02E13, Maximum Likelihood and Why Loss Functions Look the Way They Do): Justify the loss function for a given prediction task from its noise assumptions.
- **Module 4 — Statistics: Evidence and Experiments** (closing episode S02E16, Correlation, Causation, Bias, and Statistical Traps): Spot confounding and selection effects in an ML dataset.
- **Module 5 — Information Theory and Numerical Computing** (closing episode S02E18, Numerical Precision: Floating Point, Overflow, and Stable Softmax): Explain and fix a NaN-from-softmax failure and choose precisions with understanding.
