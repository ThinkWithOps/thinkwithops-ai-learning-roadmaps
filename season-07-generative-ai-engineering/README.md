## Introduction

Teach how to build dependable applications on top of LLMs: API mechanics, prompting, structured output, context management, application architecture, reliability, cost, multimodal and voice, image/video/audio generation engineering, customization choices, evaluation and guardrails. Framework and vendor specifics are treated as interchangeable examples of durable patterns.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Working with LLMs Programmatically** (closing episode S07E05, Context Management: Windows, Memory, Summarization, and Token Budgets): Implement a context policy that keeps cost bounded and quality stable.
- **Module 2 — Application Architecture and Reliability** (closing episode S07E10, Orchestration Frameworks: What They Abstract and When Not to Use Them): Make and defend a build-versus-framework decision.
- **Module 3 — Multimodal, Customization, and Model Choice** (closing episode S07E16, Foundation Model Providers and Their APIs: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Open-Weight Families): Select models and providers for a use case from capability and constraint evidence, and design an application-side integration layer that can route, fall back and control cost without rewriting application code.
- **Module 4 — Generative Media Engineering** (closing episode S07E18, Video and Audio Generation Engineering): Choose and integrate video or audio generation capabilities with realistic cost, latency, quality and policy controls.
- **Module 5 — Quality, Safety, and Productization** (closing episode S07E21, Building a Production GenAI Service: End-to-End Blueprint): Produce a launch-ready design for a GenAI feature and defend its trade-offs.
