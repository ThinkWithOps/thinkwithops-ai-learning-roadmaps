## Introduction

Move from "I can fit a model" to "I can deliver a trustworthy model on messy real problems": imbalance, uncertainty, time, ranking, anomalies, causal questions, automation (AutoML), explainability, fairness and system design. This is the ML-engineer core before deep learning.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Reliable Predictions on Hard Data** (closing episode S04E03, Hyperparameter Optimization and Experiment Discipline): Design a bounded, reproducible tuning study and judge whether a gain is real.
- **Module 2 — Advanced Tabular Modeling** (closing episode S04E07, Ensembling: Stacking, Blending, and Model Combination): Build a leak-free stack and decide whether its gain justifies its cost.
- **Module 3 — Time Series and Forecasting** (closing episode S04E09, Forecasting Models: ARIMA, Gradient Boosting, and Deep Approaches): Select and justify a forecasting approach and produce honest intervals.
- **Module 4 — Recommendation and Ranking Systems** (closing episode S04E11, Ranking Systems: Candidate Generation, Rankers, and Evaluation): Describe a production ranking pipeline and evaluate it with appropriate metrics.
- **Module 5 — Specialized Problems and Responsible ML** (closing episode S04E15, Fairness, Bias, and Responsible ML): Audit a model by subgroup and explain the trade-offs in a mitigation choice.
- **Module 6 — Experimentation, Causality, Automation, and System Design** (closing episode S04E21, ML System Design: From Problem to Production Blueprint): Produce a structured design document for an ML system and defend its trade-offs.
