## Introduction

Teach retrieval-augmented generation as an information-retrieval system with a language model at the end: ingestion, chunking, embeddings, indexes, hybrid retrieval, reranking, grounded generation, evaluation, debugging, security and production operation. Vector-database and framework products are examples of patterns, not the subject.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Building the Knowledge Index** (closing episode S08E04, Embedding Models: Selection, Dimensions, and Domain Fit): Compare embedding models on a task-specific retrieval test set.
- **Module 2 — Retrieval Engineering** (closing episode S08E08, Reranking and Query Transformation): Design a two-stage retrieval pipeline and justify each added stage with measurements.
- **Module 3 — Generation, Context, and Advanced Patterns** (closing episode S08E14, Multimodal and Domain-Specific RAG: Images, Tables, and Code): Adapt ingestion and retrieval to non-text and specialized domains.
- **Module 4 — Evaluation, Production, and Security** (closing episode S08E19, Enterprise RAG Reference Architecture): Present a defensible enterprise RAG architecture with operations and governance.
