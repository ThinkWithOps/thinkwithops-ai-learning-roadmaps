## Introduction

Bring everything together at production and enterprise level: system design and reliability for AI, AI-specific security, privacy, responsible AI, regulation and risk, AIOps (AI applied to operations), incident management, economics, and reference architectures and three end-to-end case studies.

## Learning Outcomes

One outcome per module, taken from the closing episode of that module. Every episode's own outcome is listed in the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

- **Module 1 — Architecture and Reliability** (closing episode S14E04, Resilience at Scale: Multi-Region Architecture, Disaster Recovery, and Data Sovereignty): Create a DR and regional strategy covering all AI system components.
- **Module 2 — AI Security and Privacy** (closing episode S14E09, Privacy and Data Protection in AI Systems): Create a privacy plan covering data used in training, retrieval and operation.
- **Module 3 — Responsible AI, Governance, and Risk** (closing episode S14E13, AI Assurance: Audit-Ready Evidence and Continuous Compliance): Specify automated evidence generation and control monitoring for AI governance.
- **Module 4 — Operations, Economics, and Enterprise Architecture** (closing episode S14E20, Case Study: Designing AI-Assisted Operations (AIOps and Agents)): Present and defend an AI-assisted operations design with safe automation boundaries and a plan to measure benefit.
